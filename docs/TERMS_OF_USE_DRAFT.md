## 1. What MatricQuest SA is
A study companion for the CAPS curriculum, built for South African Grade 12 learners. It is not a
Department of Basic Education (DBE) product, is not endorsed by the DBE, and does not guarantee any
examination outcome.

## 2. Accounts
You may use Guest Mode without an account. Creating an account requires accurate information for
the fields we ask (§2 of the Privacy Policy) and that you keep your credentials confidential. You
are responsible for activity under your account except where caused by our security failure.

## 3. Content accuracy and official sources
- We source curriculum information from official DBE publications where possible and record the
  source and retrieval date against imported content (see `docs/DATA_MODEL.md`). Content is marked
  with a verification status; content flagged "unverified" or "demo" has not yet been confirmed
  against an official source and must not be treated as authoritative.
- Past examination papers and memoranda are provided for study purposes, sourced from or linked to
  official DBE publications. Where we cannot confirm redistribution rights, we link to the official
  source instead of hosting the file (see `docs/DATA_MODEL.md#past-papers`).
- **Quest Coach** is an AI study aid. It is instructed to answer only from reviewed material and to
  say when it cannot verify an answer, but AI-generated responses can still be wrong. Quest Coach's
  output is guidance, not an official answer, and is never a substitute for your teacher or the
  official memorandum.
- **Essay/extended-response AI feedback is guidance only** and is explicitly not equivalent to
  official examination marking. We label it as such everywhere it appears in the app.

## 4. Mastery estimates and readiness scores
Figures the app calls "mastery estimates" or "readiness scores" describe how you have performed on
practice material inside the app. They are **not predictions or guarantees of your final NSC
results**, which depend on many factors outside this app's knowledge.

`docs/THREAT_MODEL.md`.
